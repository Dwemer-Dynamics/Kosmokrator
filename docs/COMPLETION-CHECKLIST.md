# Completion checklist

Use this checklist before delivering a nation, map or content revision. Apply the
relevant checks to the saved result, not an earlier draft. Record deliberate
exceptions, missing evidence and user-requested prototype/reuse scope. Repeat
only checks affected by subsequent changes.

1. **Brief and mechanics:** reconcile requested content, recruitment, IDs, forms,
   paths, spell access and descriptions against saved definitions. Apply the
   [nation quality gates](NATION-DESIGN.md#generation-quality-gates) or
   [map design gates](MAP-DESIGN.md). Check leadership, movement, progression,
   inherited abilities and transformations where applicable.
2. **Assets:** reconcile every required sprite pair, flag, banner and item icon
   against the asset checklist. Review native size, format, transparency, alignment
   and equipment consistency. Record provenance and explicit reuse exceptions;
   missing required artwork is unfinished work. Follow [asset rules](ASSET-RULESET.md).
3. **Sounds:** audit final weapon and spell assignments, including inherited
   samples and changed loadouts. Record suitable defaults or unresolved choices;
   check custom-file references and packaging if used. Follow [sound rules](SOUND-DESIGN.md).
4. **Compatibility and balance:** check declared dependencies, ID conflicts,
   overrides/load order, costs, equipment, magic and summon efficiency. Report
   catalog version and unresolved references. Static comparisons are not combat
   simulations or proof of multiplayer compatibility.
5. **Validation:** run supported checks after saving. Fix errors; explain warnings
   and list unverified mechanics. A clean result covers only supported checks.
6. **Maps:** review province ownership, adjacency, starts, land/sea budgets,
   playable planes and requested seasonal/terrain variants. Inspect image alignment
   and staged review revisions before guarded acceptance. Follow [map jobs](AGENT-MAPS.md).
7. **Export:** produce a fresh export from the reviewed revision. Confirm required
   files and references, asset/source hashes and manifest agreement. Keep local
   catalogs, native caches, private reference snapshots, prompts and scratch files
   out of game payloads. Record exact project, revision and export paths.
8. **Evidence and handoff:** report completed content, remaining issues and where
   to open the project. Keep static, visual, packaging, browser, engine-load and
   gameplay results separate. Run game/listening checks only within authorized
   scope and available tools; if skipped or blocked, say so without blocking a
   statically complete delivery or inventing evidence. Do not claim gameplay
   balance or sound playback was tested from file inspection.

Maintain a project-local progress record with planned/completed/unfinished work,
next resumable step, validation and export identities. For nations, include the
brief, references and prioritized review findings required by
[Nation design](NATION-DESIGN.md#review-output).
